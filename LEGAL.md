## What happens to search data

- **External requests:** selected lookups send the identifiers and request data
  needed for the search to third-party websites or APIs. Depending on enabled
  features, requests may also involve search engines, DNS services and enrichment
  providers. Recipients can receive your connection's IP address and request
  metadata; configured proxies and external providers have their own practices.
- **Your device:** configuration, saved reports and optional search history are
  stored locally. Configuration may contain API credentials. Recent-search
  history is off by default; enabling it retains up to ten saved searches. Use
  its delete/clear controls to remove saved entries. Exported reports, terminal
  output, shell history, backups and copies you share are separate and are not
  removed by clearing Consensus history. Local files are not automatically
  encrypted by Consensus.
- **Updates and installation:** online downloads and update checks contact the
  configured distribution and dependency providers. Those requests expose normal
  connection metadata but do not include your search queries or results as update
  data. Startup checks can be disabled with `CONSENSUS_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1`.
- **AI connections:** if you enable MCP, connected clients can request searches
  and retrieve results. Their operators or AI providers may process that data
  under their own policies. Choose clients and access settings accordingly.
- **Contact and contributions:** information you deliberately send to the Society
  is received through the channel you choose. Do not submit credentials or private
  investigation material. Issues, contributions and Git metadata may become
  public and persist in redistributed copies; use an appropriate public identity
  and GitHub no-reply email address.

Running a local search does not automatically submit a case report to the Society.
This section describes the distributed software, not a promise that third parties
cannot observe requests. Website visits, membership and contact-form processing
are covered separately by the [website privacy policy](https://osint.ngo/privacy-policy).
