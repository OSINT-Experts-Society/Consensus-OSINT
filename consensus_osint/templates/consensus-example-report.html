One platform. The strength of many.

Consensus combines lookup modules and site lists from major OSINT tools into one reverse lookup platform for emails, usernames and phone numbers. Its rules and algorithms reconcile overlapping checks, assess evidence strength and bring available account and profile details into one clear view, helping you identify the strongest leads.

What is compared?

Compares returned names, usernames, biography text, contact details, locations and organizations. Background analysis, checking flags, counters, URLs, generic page titles/descriptions and exact query echoes are excluded. Word matching uses words of at least three letters and an English list of common words to ignore; it does not translate text or infer identity. Queries retain their exact recorded text, including case; distinct queries do not necessarily refer to distinct accounts or people. Sites are grouped by their recorded provider or app identity, falling back to the result URL host or site name. Checks identified only by a shared API host are excluded from comparisons because their provider identity is unresolved. Recognized fields are inspected up to six levels deep, with at most 64 fields per result and 4,096 characters per field.
