sherlockV2 site corpus — attribution
------------------------------------

The detection signatures in `sherlockv2/resources/sites.json` /
`sites.yaml` (error type, error message indicators, probe/API URLs,
`username_claimed` samples, and HTTP method for POST-based platforms)
are derived from the MIT-licensed data published by the
[sherlock-project/sherlock](https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock)
project (`data.json`).

Sherlock is distributed under the MIT License. A copy of that license is
below. sherlockV2 republishes the *facts* (URLs and HTML/JSON response
markers) extracted from it; no sherlock source code is copied.

The remainder of the corpus — display names, categories, priorities,
rate limits, profile-data extractors, and the ~110 platforms curated
in the original sherlockV2 tool — is original work.
