# Community Code of Conduct

Consensus is maintained by **OSINT GROUP LTD** (company number 16684773), operating
as the **OSINT Experts Society**. See [legal and privacy information](LEGAL.md).
We welcome people of all
backgrounds and experience levels, and expect everyone to treat others with
respect.

## Our standards

- Be constructive, considerate and patient. Critique ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal identifiers, investigation targets,
  credentials or sensitive case material in public issues or discussions.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, stalk or publish someone's private
  information without permission.
- Do not use project spaces to organise abuse or unauthorised targeting.
- Follow maintainer guidance and respect requests to stop inappropriate behaviour.

These standards apply to project issues, discussions, pull requests and other
community spaces managed by the Society, including when representing Consensus.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the **OSINT Experts Society** through [osint.ngo](https://osint.ngo/contact-us).
Avoid posting sensitive allegations or personal details in a public issue.
Share only the information needed to explain the concern.

## Moderation

Maintainers will consider the context and severity of reported behaviour. They
may request changes, remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. They will aim to handle reports discreetly
and fairly. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
