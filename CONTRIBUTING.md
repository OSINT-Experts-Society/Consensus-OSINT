# Contributing to Consensus

Help improve Consensus by reporting a problem, suggesting a lookup source,
improving documentation or proposing a focused change.

## Report a problem

Open an issue with your Consensus version, operating system, expected behaviour
and what happened. Include a minimal example and relevant error messages where
possible. Remove personal identifiers, credentials and sensitive investigation
data before sharing logs or reports.

## Suggest a lookup source

Link to the service or upstream project, describe whether it supports username,
email or phone lookups, and explain what useful information it provides. Mention
any account, API-key or payment requirements you know about.

## Propose a change

For a substantial change, open an issue first so maintainers can discuss the
approach. Keep pull requests focused, explain the problem and resulting behaviour,
and describe how you checked the change. Use synthetic examples or fixtures;
do not include real investigation targets or private data.

Preserve upstream credits and licence notices. Contributions are reviewed by
the maintainers before inclusion; submitting a proposal does not guarantee acceptance.

## Community

Follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). For community enquiries,
visit the [OSINT Experts Society](https://osint.ngo).
